How many photos do University of Maryland Athletics Photographers take?

By Austin DeSisto

April 19, 2026

Hundreds of athletics events happen each month at the University of Maryland. These include games, practices and some corporate events. This leads to an overwhleming amount of photos which must be edited and cataloged by the team of photographers. Photo totals stem from a variety of factors such as the amount of events in a given month and the length of such events. Not directly included in this data is the type of event and the photographer, which are other variables to be considered.
